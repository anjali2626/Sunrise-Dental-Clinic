Sunrise Dental Clinic Management System

Use the navigation menu on the left to access the different clinic management functions.

Appointments

Use the Appointments page to create and manage patient appointments, including dentist, treatment, date and time information.

Patients

Use the Patients page to search and manage patient records.

Billing

The Billing page is used to create bills based on consultation fees and treatment costs.

Reports

The Reports page provides daily appointment reports and treatment/revenue summaries.
